# Preliminary Problem Synthesis

**Source documents:** all files in `docs/discovery/` (interview note, problem hypotheses, hypothesis priority, interview guide, evidence matrix, segment hypotheses, problem-space map).
**Status: PRELIMINARY** — based on a single interview. Hypotheses and assumptions are labeled; nothing below is a validated finding. No product features are proposed and no target market is locked.

---

## 1. Confirmed observations

From one interview (father-in-law — landscape architect / landscape & plant business):
- Project value (omzet) can reach hundreds of millions of rupiah per project.
- Administration is recorded mostly manually.
- Administrative recording errors have occurred.
- Evidence base: one interview; no documents, ledgers, or error samples were provided or reviewed.

## 2. Strongest current problem hypothesis

**H1 (hypothesis, unvalidated):** manual recording is more error-prone than a structured process, especially as activity volume grows.
- Ranked first in `hypothesis-priority.md` (preliminary score 19/25, vs H3 = 16, H2 = 15) because exposure is total (all administration is manual) and it is the easiest hypothesis to validate across businesses.
- Note: H1 leads on exposure and testability, not on confirmed causation — the actual root cause of the observed errors (H2) is still unknown.

## 3. Evidence supporting it

- All administration is manual, so every entry is exposed to manual-entry risk (confirmed fact).
- Errors have occurred (confirmed fact) — consistent with H1, but the interview does not establish that manual recording caused them.
- High project values raise the potential stakes per error (confirmed fact; amplification itself is hypothesis H3).
- No direct evidence yet: no error samples, frequencies, amounts, or root-cause analysis exist in the documents.

## 4. What is still unknown

- Project count, pricing, and record types kept; manual media used; who records entries (interview note unknowns).
- Error types, frequency, amounts, how errors are discovered and corrected.
- Time or financial impact of errors (evidence matrix: all "Unknown / Not yet validated").
- Whether any of this generalizes beyond the single interviewed business.

## 5. Assumptions to validate before finalizing the product

- **A1 (H1):** manual recording is the error mechanism — not process ownership or missing verification (H2).
- **A2 (H3):** errors are material relative to project value (no loss amounts documented yet).
- **A3:** error frequency and impact are high enough to matter — no numbers exist yet.
- **A4:** the pattern extends beyond one business — wedding vendors and UI/UX/creative agencies are untested candidate segments.
- **A5:** software can realistically reduce these errors in this context (scored 4/5 for H1, unproven).

## 6. Businesses that may potentially share the problem

- UI/UX or creative agencies (candidate segment in the interview guide; zero interviews).
- Scope is high-value, project-based businesses per the interview guide; the target market remains open until more interviews are conducted.
